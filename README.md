# Business-Assets
Public facing repository showcasing business cases and product briefs for personal projects

DealParser AI: AI powered rent roll and T-12 parsing for small and independent real estate investors. Upload a PDF, get a verified pro forma in minutes instead of hours. DealParse AI is a product and business case explores a real problem I felt in during a Real Estate elective at U of Wisconsin MBA program. The following MVP code I generated by prompt engineering through v0 app. The focus of the project is not necessarily a fully functioning business (it would be great) but more of a demonstration of my ability to think through product innovation, value proposition, competitive analysis, go-to-market analysis, and business case.

UI demo: https://dealparserai.vercel.app/
